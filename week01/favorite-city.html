My favorite city: CUENCA - ECUADOR

Cuenca is my favorite city. It has beautiful mountains and is located approximately 2,600 meters above sea level. The weather is generally cool and pleasant.

What I love most about Cuenca is its architecture, especially the historic churches and traditional buildings. The city has four beautiful rivers that cross through it, and it is known for being a clean and peaceful city.

Another thing I love about Cuenca is its traditional food. I especially enjoy cuy and colada morada, which are two of the city's most popular traditional dishes.
